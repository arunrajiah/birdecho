BirdEcho connects to the bird station you already run (BirdNET-Pi, BirdNET-Go or BirdWeather) and shows what it hears: live detections, songs, species histories and statistics, on your iPhone.

BirdEcho is not a bird-identification app. It does not use your microphone or run a detection model: it shows what your own station has already detected. No station yet? Try the built-in demo station with sample data.

Features:
- Live detection feed with confidence scores, refreshed every minute
- Listen to each detection and share it as an image card
- Species browser with each species' history and recent sightings
- Rarity badge for birds your station seldom hears, and optional rare-species alerts
- Favourites, with a notification when a favourite is heard (once a day per species)
- 14-day chart and headline stats: today, total, unique species
- Export detections as CSV
- Offline: the last 24 hours stay readable without a connection
- Several stations: save as many as you like and switch in Settings
- Station map for BirdWeather stations
- Nearby arrival dates and species pages from Wildlife Network
- Dark mode and station-time timestamps

Connections:
- BirdNET-Pi and BirdNET-Go: directly over your home network, no cloud account
- BirdWeather: through the BirdWeather API with your station token (kept in the iOS Keychain)

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Free and open source: github.com/arunrajiah/birdecho
